2. Description of Service

RepCast is a digital subscription platform designed for personal trainers and physiotherapists. It provides tools to:

  • Browse a curated master library of exercises with video demonstrations
  • Upload custom exercise videos to build a personal library
  • Build personalised client exercise routines with sets, reps, and coaching notes
  • Share routines with clients via a unique link (no client login required)

The service is provided entirely digitally. No physical goods are shipped. Access is granted immediately upon successful subscription.
